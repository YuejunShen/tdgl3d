**Device geometry and boundary conditions for the 3D scalar TDGL model.**
**a,** An ideal-electrode/dielectric/BaTiO₃ (BTO)/dielectric/ideal-electrode capacitor with ferroelectric thickness h_f = 50 nm and two dielectric layers of thickness h_d = 1 nm each. The background relative permittivities are ε_f = 600 and ε_d = 300. Polarization is restricted to the out-of-plane direction, P_FE = P(x,y,z,t) ẑ; the illustrated 180° domains are schematic rather than simulation results. L_x and L_y denote the lateral dimensions.
**b,** The lower electrode is held at φ(z = −h_d) = V(t), and the upper electrode at φ(z = h_f + h_d) = 0. Both P and φ are periodic in x and y. At the BTO faces, the polarization obeys the Robin condition ∂P/∂n = −P/λ, where n is the outward normal from BTO and λ_b = λ_t = 5 nm. Equivalently, ∂P/∂z = +P/λ_b at the bottom face z = 0 and ∂P/∂z = −P/λ_t at the top face z = h_f. The extrapolation lengths describe polarization suppression at the surfaces and are independent of the dielectric thicknesses. At each BTO/dielectric interface, φ and the normal displacement D_z are continuous because there is no free interface charge. Bound polarization charge is included through D = ε₀εE + χ_f P ẑ, with E = −∇φ and ∇·D = 0. Here χ_f is one in BTO and zero in the dielectrics. The dielectric layers and their thicknesses are modeling assumptions; the geometry is not drawn to scale.
